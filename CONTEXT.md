# PeteTongIsAI

A fan-made DJ-desk website where visitors play music, trigger Pete Tong phrases, and send friends personalised music links introduced in Pete's (synthesised) voice.

## Language

**Dedication**:
A shareable link carrying an Intro written by whoever sent it, optionally followed by a Spotify track or playlist.
_Avoid_: Share, link, shout-out, request

**Fade**:
When a Dedication stops being available, either because a visitor removed it or because nobody opened it within the inactivity window.
_Avoid_: Expire, delete, takedown

**Intro**:
The spoken part of a Dedication, heard before the music starts.
_Avoid_: Voiceover, message audio

**Track ID**:
Pete's spoken back-announcement naming the last two tracks that played, most recent first, in the pause before the next one starts.
_Avoid_: Announcement, now-playing, shout

**Sign-off**:
A fixed line in Pete's voice at the end of every Dedication, inviting the listener to hand over a playlist for Track IDs.
_Avoid_: Outro, call to action

**Reference Clip**:
A short recording of Pete's real speech that the voice model imitates.
_Avoid_: Sample, training clip, voice prompt

**Jingle**:
A fixed, original phrase written for the site, in Pete's voice, triggered by a button on the desk.
_Avoid_: Drop, catchphrase, sound effect
