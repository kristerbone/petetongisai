# Spotify embeds, not the Web Playback SDK

Music plays through Spotify embeds (driven by the iFrame API), not the Web Playback SDK, even though that means the desk can't control or mix the actual audio. A Dedication must play for any recipient, but a Web Playback SDK app in development mode is limited to 5 allowlisted Spotify Premium users, and since May 2025 extended quota is only granted to registered businesses with 250k+ monthly active users.

## Consequences

- The turntables are decorative: they reflect embed playback state but don't control it.
- Recipients not signed into Spotify in their browser hear 30-second previews.
